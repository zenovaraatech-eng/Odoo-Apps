ODOO 18.0  •  POINT OF SALE

PoS Numpad Collapse Toggle

Maximize your Point of Sale screen real estate with a single, elegant click.

Reclaim Your Screen Space

Cashiers handling large customer orders often run out of vertical space on the standard Odoo POS screen. This module introduces a highly responsive, animated toggle button right above the numpad. Hide the numpad when you want to review the full order list, and slide it back seamlessly whenever you need to enter amounts.

Premium Features

01

One-Click Toggle

Instantly collapse or expand the entire numpad via an elegantly placed, intuitive chevron button on the interface.

02

Native Auto-Reset

Plays perfectly with standard Odoo logic. When an order is emptied or deselected, the interface resets automatically.

03

Zero Performance Drop

Optimized rendering ensures that your Point of Sale remains lightning fast, with zero lag during toggle operations.

See It In Action

STEP 1

Expand / Collapse Toggle

While processing an order, notice the new toggle button hovering gracefully above your standard numpad. A single click initiates the collapse.

STEP 2

Clean, Open Interface

The numpad tucks away neatly, expanding your cart view so you can easily review dozens of items at a glance without scrolling endlessly.
